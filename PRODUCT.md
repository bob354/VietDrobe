# VietDrobe

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Young Vietnamese visitors exploring traditional dress for events, photography, and personal styling, then arranging a rental.

## Product Purpose
Help people discover Vietnamese traditional costumes, compose an outfit with styling assistance, and rent selected garments.

## Positioning
The catalog, AI stylist, mix-and-match studio, cultural checks, and rental checkout form one connected journey.

## Operating Context
Visitors browse a garment catalog, filter and inspect pieces, request three outfit suggestions, assemble a look in the studio, and submit a rental booking.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Preserve the existing Next.js frontend and FastAPI endpoints.
- Browsing and renting, and AI styling and the studio, have equal prominence.
- The interface remains in Vietnamese and supports light and dark themes.
- No AI-generated imagery may appear in the web app. Existing garment artwork may be used.

## Brand Commitments
- Use the name VietDrobe throughout the interface.
- The visual tone is minimal and traditional with a restrained modern touch, informed by the user's references.

## Evidence on Hand
The repository contains garment data and illustrations, working catalog and styling flows, rental state, and API definitions. There are no provided product photographs or testimonials.

## Product Principles
- Show the garment and its cultural context before decoration.
- Make both direct rental and guided styling easy to begin.
- Keep rental choices and totals clear at every step.
- Respect Vietnamese cultural specificity in words and imagery.
